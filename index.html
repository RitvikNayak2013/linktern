Is Linktern really free?
Yes. Linktern is completely free for students, early-career folks, clubs, programs and schools. You get the full product with no billing, no credit card and no hidden upgrade wall.
Can I use it if I am still in high school?
Absolutely. Many of our earliest users are high school students exploring research labs, startups and online projects. We help you filter for opportunities that make sense at your level.
Which fields does Linktern support?
We are starting with STEM, design, product, research and content roles. Our focus is on internships where learning, mentorship and real experience are built in.
Can clubs, schools or programs use it as well?
Yes. If you run a club, program or school and want a better way for students to discover and track opportunities, Linktern is free for you too. Reach out and we will help you onboard your cohort.
